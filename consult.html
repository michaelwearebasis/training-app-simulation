The best results start with a pre-quote conversation.

Sit down with the customer before you quote — each answer changes how you set up the board.

What to ask — and why
1

What do they want from it?

Insights only, or control and automation too? It decides how far you separate loads on the board.

2

What should get its own circuit?

Anything they'll want to see, control or automate — HWC, EV charger, pool or spa, oven, heat pumps. Grouped loads can't be automated well.

3

What must never switch off?

Fridge, freezer, security, router. These get standby lockout — everything else stays controllable.

4

How will it connect?

Ethernet to the board if you can. Poor Wi-Fi is the #1 problem.

5

Is the existing wiring up to it?

Condition, capacity, supply limits — flag anything below standard now, not on install day.
